# Codex PDF to EPUB

Use Codex to convert PDFs to EPUBs, complete with an instruction set containing extensive generalisable corrections, including incorrect punctuation marks, line breaks, italicisation, bolding, and so on. Simply clone this repo, pull in the source PDF, run `codex`, and tell it to convert it to an EPUB, and it will automatically read the extensive instructions in [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/blob/main/AGENTS.md) to overcome most common (and some uncommon) pitfalls. It will also automatically update its own instructions when it decides any solutions it comes up with or any corrections you specify are generalisable to future conversions.

- You should always try to source the cleanest, best-quality PDFs you can. Reasonably clean book scans are okay. [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/refs/heads/main/examples/You%20Can%20Be%20Right%20-%20Dana%20Adam%20Shapiro.epub) is the EPUB generated from *[You Can Be Right (or You Can Be Married)](https://www.amazon.com/You-Can-Be-Right-Married/dp/1451657781)* from a reasonably clean book scan. You can find the source PDF (and my EPUB) on [Anna's Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna's_Archive).
- This also works for books and essays that have been made available online in HTML, in which case you can just use a URL as the source. [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/refs/heads/main/examples/The%20Story%20of%20Zoya%20and%20Shura%20-%20L%20Kosmodemyanskaya.epub) is the EPUB generated from *[The Story of Zoya and Shura](https://gammacloud.org/features/zoya/story-zoya-shura/)* in this fashion.
- Wherever possible, you should first see if there already is a good EPUB option available: many academic journals permit you to download articles in EPUB format. Then you can smooth out the rough edges: if there's no cover, you can generate one; if there's residual errors, you can correct them. [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/refs/heads/main/examples/The%20experience%20of%20drowning%20-%20Michael%20Tipton%2C%20Hugh%20Montgomery.epub) is the cleaned-up EPUB generated from the EPUB of *[The experience of drowning](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00258172211053127)* on Sage Journals.
- Tables, graphs, diagrams, images, and mathematical equations can all be converted neatly, but only if they are sequenced in a relatively linear, clear-cut fashion. [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/refs/heads/main/examples/What%20Predicts%20Divorce%20-%20John%20Mordechai%20Gottman.epub) is the EPUB generated from a clean, mostly plaintext-based PDF of *[What Predicts Divorce](https://www.amazon.com/What-Predicts-Divorce-Relationship-Processes/dp/0805814027)*, containing plenty of tables and statistics. You can find the source PDF (and my EPUB) on [Anna's Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna's_Archive).
- Sometimes, a useful but imperfect transcription is available alongside a more authoritative source. For example, you may have a good EPUB of an older edition of a book, and a scanned PDF of a newer edition. Alternatively, you may have an imperfect cleaned-up OCR PDF plus a more authoritative scanned PDF source. In such instances, you can provide both for a more reliable conversion. [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/refs/heads/main/examples/Ukraine%20and%20Russia%20-%20Paul%20D%27Anieri.epub) is the EPUB generated from *[Ukraine and Russia](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/ukraine-and-russia/EE1BF8E4D463FBACD0CCEF52A7B1CF69)* from an excellent EPUB of an older edition combined with an authoritative PDF of a newer edition, both of which you can find on [Anna's Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna's_Archive) (my new EPUB is also there).
- Certain books that are heavily dependent on formatting and imagery, such as textbooks or books on fashion, may require much more book-specific instructions, or even not be worth converting. A little bit of creativity goes a long way! [Here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JakeHPark/Codex-PDF-to-EPUB/refs/heads/main/examples/Postural%20Disorders%20and%20Musculoskeletal%20Dysfunction%20-%20Gill%20Solberg.epub) is the EPUB generated from *[Postural Disorders and Musculoskeletal Dysfunction](https://www.amazon.com/Postural-Disorders-Musculoskeletal-Dysfunction-Prevention/dp/0443103828)*, a textbook with lots of images, diagrams and notes in all sorts of places—left, right and centre.
- I like to use the Sol variant of GPT on the Medium mode as a good balance between cost and efficacy.
